Prepare a target, not a generic search

Role family
What work?

Data science, data engineering, ML, or streaming systems.

Level
What scope?

Choose a realistic level and expected ownership.

Work type
What setting?

Product, platform, research, consulting, or operations.

Constraints
What limits?

Location, work model, authorization, timing, and non-negotiables.

Evaluate the written offer before negotiating

RoleResponsibilities and success criteria
Manager + teamSupport, working style, and stability
Scope + growthOwnership, learning, and progression
CompensationBase, bonus, equity, and benefits
ConditionsLocation, start date, deadline, and contingencies
Risk + alternativesTradeoffs, uncertainty, and Plan B

Compare written facts. Do not trade a written term for a vague future promise.

Negotiate, confirm the terms, then sign

Negotiate

Make one prioritized request

1Set priorities and limits.
2Make a specific, evidence-based request.
3Compare the tradeoff and response.
4Keep a realistic Plan B.
→
Sign

Accept the final written terms

1Review written terms and deadline.
2Resolve conditions and open questions.
3Confirm every agreed change in writing.
4Sign, receive confirmation, and close other processes.
